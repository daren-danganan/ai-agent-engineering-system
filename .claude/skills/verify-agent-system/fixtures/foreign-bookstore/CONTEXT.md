# Context

Glossary for Corner Books. Definitions only.

## Glossary

- **Title**: a book as published, identified by ISBN. Several copies can share one title.
- **Copy**: one physical book on the shelf or in the back room.
- **Special order**: a title a customer asked for that is not in stock; it becomes a copy on arrival and is held for that customer.
- **Hold shelf**: where copies for special orders wait until picked up. A copy leaves the hold shelf after 14 days unclaimed.
